# Artist website

## Register
brand

## Users
Listeners arriving from a social profile, looking for this artist's music, releases, listening activity, and contact links.

## Product Purpose
A self-hosted artist home for c4milo, with a link hub, featured Querida release and a gallery of floating cover links. The original objective included listening activity; the user subsequently removed the entire listening and About region, so those features are now disabled. The current design is shaped by browser annotations: compact cover, no header navigation or featured date, minimal tape-loop audio player and six white platform logos. Missing artwork, audio and destinations remain placeholders as authorized. The logo is still being made.

The user owns c4milo.com through Domain.com and is unsure of hosting cost. The launch guide now prices a user-owned VPS at $6/month plus optional backups, with a free managed static-host alternative clearly distinguished. No deployment or account changes have been made. The cover is supplied locally by the user; do not inspect its image content or include it in screenshots without explicit permission. The full personalized launch remains incomplete.

## Brand Personality
Reference-led restraint: intimate, spare, music-first. Final identity remains undecided.

## Design Principles
- Put music and destination links within immediate reach.
- Let artwork carry the artist's identity once provided.
- Keep the featured release, catalogue and platform destinations easy to find.
- Label all missing content and demo listening data honestly.
- Keep source, domain, server, and credentials under the artist's ownership.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, responsive mobile layout. WCAG AA is an implementation target, not a certification.

Minimal visible navigation; spacious vertical composition; moody performance photography and red vinyl product imagery; embedded update signup, linked vinyl artwork, and stacked YouTube videos.
